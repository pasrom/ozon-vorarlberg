What the assessment is based on

Two separate axes, deliberately not mixed. The training light runs on the current 1-hour value — that is what you are breathing right now. The daily assessment runs on the daily maximum of the running 8-hour mean and is the health context of the day.

The 100 and 120 marks of the training scale are borrowed from the 8-hour guidelines, making it a pragmatic scale rather than a legal limit. 180 µg/m³ is the actual Austrian information threshold (1-hour mean).

Personal project, not an official warning. This page is run by a private individual and is not a publication of the Province of Vorarlberg. The training light is an own, pragmatic reading and carries no legal weight. For threshold breaches and in any case of doubt, only vorarlberg-luft.at applies. Anyone with respiratory complaints is better served by a doctor than by a dashboard.

Source: vorarlberg-luft.at (Province of Vorarlberg, Umweltinstitut) · all values in µg/m³ · times in local time (Europe/Vienna).
